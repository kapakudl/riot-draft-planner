Scoring method and limitations

After B1: score starts at 50. Overall smoothed win rate contributes only 10 × (rate − 0.5), at most ±5 points. Ally and opponent evidence each contribute 45 × mean matchup lift. We estimate an ally baseline by adding the two champions’ smoothed log-odds; for opponents we subtract their log-odds. Lift = (observed wins − games × baseline) / (games + 20). This shrinks small samples toward zero. Missing evidence adds nothing. At least one current pairing needs five games to qualify; minimum total games and role feasibility also apply.

Opening B1: 65 × Wilson 95% lower bound on observed B1 wins + 25 × square root of (B1 appearances / most frequent B1 appearances) + 10 × (observed roles − 1) / 4. Priority measures actual first-pick adoption; it is not a win-probability estimate. Role options require at least 5% historical usage. All weights are explicit heuristics, not fitted or validated model parameters.

Search evaluates the full champion roster, including unranked candidates. Inspect shows rank, score components, sample counts, baseline expectations, adjusted matchup lifts, role fit, and exclusion reasons. Searching or inspecting never locks a pick. Pick explicitly to advance.

Pair results overlap; sample counts must not be added as independent games. Opponent presence is not a lane counter measure. Baselines do not control professional team/player strength, schedule, draft intent, or patch changes within an All patches filter. These data cannot establish causal synergy or prove an optimal composition. Use a relevant patch and league. No champion-kit, damage balance, engage, or range traits are inferred from win rates.
